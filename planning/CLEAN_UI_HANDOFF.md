# Current visual direction — clean white

5 November 2026. The owner rejected the dark luxury concept: prefers the first clean white design and much less hero text/clutter.

- Original `styles.css` remains the storefront base. `clean.css` supplies logo framing and minimal carousel styles. `luxury.css` is no longer imported.
- Supplied logo retained unchanged in header/footer.
- Hero: “Upgrade your everyday.”, one short supporting line, one shop CTA, a large product area and understated controls.
- Five-product crossfade retained, with pause, previous/next, direct selectors, keyboard/touch input, visibility/focus pausing and reduced-motion support.
- Removed hero texture, arch, orbit graphics, floating animations, decorative headings, changing promotional captions, secondary CTA and footer slogans within the hero.
- Restored original sans-serif headings, simple categories/cards and light overall layout. Hire-purchase choices remain visible below the hero and on eligible products.
- Existing backend/admin/API integration and preview commerce restrictions preserved. No deployment in this update.
